How Scores Are Calculated

Every candidate's score is a P(Hit) percentage — the estimated probability that the player gets at least one hit today. A score of 72% means the model estimates a 72% chance of a hit. This directly maps to the Beat the Streak objective.

Step 1 — Candidate Filters Applied before scoring

Players must pass both filters to be considered.

FilterThresholdWhy
Plate Appearances (last 7 days) ≥ 15 PA Excludes bench players, injured players, and too-small samples
Strikeout Rate (last 7 days) < 25% K% High K% batters make less contact; fewer official ABs hurts hit probability
Step 2 — Effective Batting Average (BA_eff)

The effective BA is a blended, adjusted measure of how likely this player is to get a hit per at-bat. Three layers of refinement:

LayerMethodRationale
1. Exponential-Decay BA weighted_BA = (H_4d × 2 + H_older) ÷ (AB_4d × 2 + AB_older) Recent games (last 4 days) are weighted 2× vs days 5–7. A batter who's hot right now scores higher than one who cooled off this week.
2. xBA Blend blended = 0.55 × xBA + 0.45 × weighted_BA Statcast xBA (expected BA based on exit velocity + launch angle) is a luck-neutral predictor. Blending 55/45 reduces noise from BABIP variance.
3. Contextual Adjustments See table below Final multipliers applied to BA_eff before the probability calculation.

Contextual adjustments (multiplicative):

FactorConditionMultiplier
Elite contact (K%)K% < 12%× 1.04
Free swinger (K%)K% > 22%× 0.97
High hard-hit rateHH% > 45%× 1.02
Low hard-hit rateHH% < 28%× 0.98
Hot teamTeam in top 10 runs scored (7d)× 1.02
Step 3 — P(Hit) Probability Formula

The goal of Beat the Streak is to pick someone who gets at least one hit. Using the Bernoulli model for independent at-bats:

P(Hit) = 1 − (1 − BA_eff)AB/G
AB/G = adjusted at-bats per game (lineup position shifts this ±0.4 ABs)

Example: A player with BA_eff = .300 and 3.6 AB/game: P(Hit) = 1 − (0.700)3.6 = ~73%

Step 4 — Platoon-Aware A-List vs B-List Split

Batters hit better against opposite-handed pitchers (platoon advantage). The split now uses actual pitcher throwing hand rather than batter handedness alone.

BatterPitcher HandPlatoonList
Left (LHB)Right (RHP) Advantage A-List
Right (RHB)Any Neutral / Adv. A-List
Left (LHB)Left (LHP) Disadvantage B-List
Left (LHB)Unknown Unverified B-List (conservative)

B-list players are not disqualified — a left-handed batter facing a right-handed pitcher should be moved to your personal A-list. Always check the [L]/[R] pitcher indicator in the matchup column.
