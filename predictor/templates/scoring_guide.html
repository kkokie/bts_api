How Scores Are Calculated

Every player is scored as a P(Hit) — the estimated probability of getting at least one hit in today's game. Scores are displayed as a percentage (e.g. 74.2%). Higher = stronger pick.

Step 1 — Candidate Filters Applied before scoring

Players must pass both filters to be considered. Weeds out bench players and free-swingers.

FilterThresholdWhy
Plate Appearances (last 7 days) ≥ 15 PA (3 early season) Excludes injured, benched, or too-small sample players. Threshold lowers in the first two weeks of the season.
Strikeout Rate (last 7 days) < 25% K% High strikeout hitters make less contact — riskier for Beat the Streak.
Step 2 — Building Effective Batting Average (BAeff)

The model doesn't use raw BA directly. It builds a layered estimate of a player's true hitting ability.

2a. Exponential-Decay Weighted BA

Recent performance matters more. The last 4 days count 2×, the prior 3 days count 1×.

BA_weighted = (H_last4d × 2 + H_days5to7) / (AB_last4d × 2 + AB_days5to7)
2b. xBA Blend (Statcast)

Expected BA (xBA) measures quality of contact and is more stable than actual BA — it strips out luck. When available, it's blended in at 55%.

BA_eff = 0.55 × xBA + 0.45 × BA_weighted
(BA_weighted used alone if xBA unavailable)

Step 3 — P(Hit): Probability of Getting a Hit

The final score is a Bernoulli probability — the chance of getting at least one hit given the expected AB count and effective BA.

P(Hit) = 1 − (1 − BA_eff) ^ AB_per_game

AB_per_game is adjusted by lineup position — leadoff hitters get ~0.4 more ABs per game than the 9-hole. The lineup position is the player's average batting order slot from Statcast data over the last 7 days.

AB_per_game_adjusted = AB_per_game + (5 − avg_order) × 0.08

Step 4 — A-List vs B-List Split

After scoring, the top candidates are split by platoon matchup. A player can score high and still land on the B-list.

ListCriteriaMeaning
A-List Cross-handed matchup (LHB vs RHP, or RHB vs LHP) Platoon advantage — recommended for Beat the Streak
B-List Same-handed matchup, or pitcher hand unknown Platoon disadvantage or uncertainty — review before picking

Pitcher hand is sourced from the MLB Stats API (announced starters) and Statcast. If a pitcher's hand is unknown, the player defaults to B-list as a precaution.

Display-Only Factors Shown in badges, not in score

These appear as badges and help you evaluate the matchup manually. They are not factored into P(Hit).
